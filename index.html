Help

Questions parents ask

What do I need to give at checkout?

The child's first name, age, and a short note: a pet, a favorite thing, something they did recently. For event packages, the children's names and a few words about the event. For mailed items, the shipping address.

How fast will it arrive?

Digital letters arrive by email in 4–5 days. Paper letters are packed in 3 business days and sent by USPS, so most arrive in about 8 days. For now we mail only to US addresses. Please order event packages at least 2 weeks before your event.

What is a MilkyCode?

Every child who gets a letter receives a personal MilkyCode: their galactic address, printed on each letter. Soon this site will have a mail register where you can enter a MilkyCode and see all the letters a child has received from the stars and sent back. Keep the code safe; each new letter is added to the same address.

Can I get a refund?

If anything arrives damaged or wrong, we reprint or refund it, your choice. Because every item is personalized, we can't accept returns for a change of mind once it's been prepared. Cancel within 12 hours of ordering for a full refund.

Is my child's information private?

We use it only to make and send your order and never share or sell it. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your card number.
